Research

Human action recognition and graph learning.

My PhD develops graph convolutional models for skeleton-based action recognition: frequency-aware topology learning (Pattern Recognition), an analysis of generalisation beyond benchmark accuracy (Computer Science Review), and ESA-GCN (ICONIP 2026), which adds language priors for at most 2.6% more parameters. First author on all eight peer-reviewed publications. Next, I plan to work where computer vision and large language models meet, from language-guided activity recognition to privacy-aware LLM systems that run on local hardware.
